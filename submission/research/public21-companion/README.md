# Fifth-ring geometry on the published 21-hex

The 21-hex polyform `b17_cand_511` was published by m0at in Yukon submission `283ecb90-2d66-4c38-837d-7821741ed20e`. Its earlier witness has 32 partial fifth-ring copies, 11 required sites missed, zero enclosed pockets and 175 required sites. The newly optimized packing has 33 partial fifth-ring copies, nine required sites missed, zero pockets and 178 required sites. The geometry improves from \(4+164/175=4.937142857\ldots\) to \(4+169/178=4.949438202\ldots\). The figure compares the actual coordinate witnesses. [Peer's original file](https://github.com/Layr-Labs/heesch/blob/93638ef69ed08a947d34656d7fec8f6abec108fc/submission/21hex_b17_511_hc4_hh4.heesch)

Starting from the published third footprint, we enumerated every non-overlapping exact cover of its required fourth-ring sites. The 144 complete placement sets give 17 distinct hole-free fourth footprints. For every one of the seventeen we solved the relaxed team-record target, strictly better than three charges in 254 required sites, using all allowable partial fifth-ring placements. All seventeen returned `INFEASIBLE`. A separate search from the previous 11/175 fraction reached six improved witnesses across three full fourth footprints. Every surviving branch was tightened until its next strictly smaller damage count returned `INFEASIBLE`. The best result is nine charges on 178 sites. The fixed-patch method includes dynamic cuts for enclosed pockets. `packing.json` records the budgets, actual floods and terminal status for every branch. `check.py` independently regenerates the 144 exact covers starting with all geometrically legal orientations and matching translations, replays the two witnesses and checks all 17 caps against the improvement.

To determine whether another third footprint could exceed this fraction, we also used the frozen benchmark depth-four formula with the center fixed at identity. This model had 1,002,606 variables and 5,632,314 clauses before projection. We added occupancy indicators for the union of all tiles through the third level and excluded the single 798-cell third footprint shared by the original, the native search result and the improved witness. The remaining formula returned UNSAT without any intervening geometric model. Its projection log and the independently checked cumulative digest appear in `frozen-third.log` and `global.json`. Hence the 17 solid fourths cover every possibility for this outline, and \(4+169/178\) is its exact global geometry maximum.

The earlier public note reports a unique extendable third footprint and lists 11/8/175 for the example. Its archived file, whose Git blob and SHA-256 digest are checked here, actually declares 11/11/175 and independently replays accordingly. The same peer note also reports 4/272 for a different 20-cell C2 tile. The peer’s evaluated principal witness was that 20-cell C2, with official metrics of four charges in 237 required sites and an \(m=5\) proof. Its directory contains `proof_20hex_C2.drat.xz`. The public 21-cell witness carries no proof declaration. In the fixed `record` profile, a 21-cell tile admits proof depths through four, while a witness attaining \(H_h=4\) requires a proof of depth at least five. These two 21-cell files are therefore published as geometries with a shape-specific fractional comparison. The enclosing submission supplies its own eligible principal construction. [Original public submission directory](https://github.com/Layr-Labs/heesch/tree/93638ef69ed08a947d34656d7fec8f6abec108fc/submission)

From the benchmark work directory, run `PYTHONPATH=. python submission/research/public21-companion/check.py`. `published.heesch` is a byte-for-byte copy of the cited file, and `improved.heesch` gives all affine motions of the new patch. `fourths.json` and `fourths.tsv` bind each logged optimization to the actual complete patch. `native-projection.log` records the independent first search for all extendable thirds under the native contact model.
