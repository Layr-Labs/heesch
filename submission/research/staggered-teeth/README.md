# Staggered teeth on a hexagon lattice

`eight-copy.heesch` contains a 102-copy patch around a 20-hex. `eight-copy-period.json` contains eight transformed copies that partition a periodic cell of area 160. The geometry and periodic cover of this example are checked by the parent `replay.py`. `staggered-teeth.png` shows the tile, an excerpt from that periodic tiling, and the finite five-ring witness.

`spines.cpp` generates separated teeth on both sides of two dense interior rows. Compile with `c++ -O3 -std=c++20 spines.cpp -o spines`. The arguments for the reported run are `(6,1)`, `(7,19)`, `(8,140)` and `(9,400)`. The first argument specifies width. The second retains a fixed fraction of the generator’s integer keys. Its 152,095 rows include both 19- and 20-cell outlines. A necessary local-contact test advances adjacencies by one depth at each pass. We included the 3,050 unresolved rows alongside 2,756 rows that passed this filter. Merging rotations and reflections gave 5,783 distinct candidates for actual five-ring placement search. The complete five-ring witness files are under the `five7`, `five8` and `five9` directories. All 20 are indexed by `five-periods.json`.

`refit.py` asks whether changing the **outline itself** can preserve a placement chart while breaking its parent’s period. Each site in the tile or its one-cell border has a binary occupancy variable. Copies in the selected chart share the same variables after applying their rigid motions. At each world site the transformed occupancies sum to at most one. At each depth, occupancy of an inner site implies occupancy of each neighbor by the following depth. For each site of the repeating domain we sum the indicators of transformed tile cells that map to its residue. At least one residue must have multiplicity different from one.

The refit limits candidates to 17–20 cells, retains at least the parent’s area minus six cells from the original tile, and uses its one-cell expanded envelope. Every returned candidate must pass the actual shape, level, contact and corona checks. On these 20 charts, the full five-ring motions and even the first two rings ruled out every period-breaking outline in those constraints. Allowing only the first ring to remain fixed gave 90 distinct verified outlines in the sampled enumeration. Native search found three five-ring witnesses among them. Independent exact covers certified all three periodic. Their witnesses and period data are included under `ref1-five` and `ref1-five-periods.json`.

From the benchmark root with OR-Tools 9.15 installed, `PYTHONPATH=. python submission/research/staggered-teeth/refit.py 5 96` reruns the full-chart test. Reruns write their outputs beside the script. A solution limit, wall-time cutoff or failure to find a periodic cover leaves the associated existence question open.
