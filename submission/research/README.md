# A construction from a finite 27-iamond to Kaplan’s four-ring 20-iamond

We found a way to cut and move pieces of the finite, three-ring 27-iamond in Attempt 20 to recover Kaplan’s 20-iamond with Heesch number four. The scored file gives a fresh 67-copy four-ring arrangement of this familiar outline and a partial fifth ring of 43 more copies. An independent contact argument in the research companion also proves that the 27-iamond cannot tile the plane. All tiles used in the scored arrangement are congruent, unmarked polyiamonds. Reflections follow the standard benchmark policy. [Kaplan’s published 20-iamond table](https://cs.uwaterloo.ca/~csk/heesch/iamond/20iamond_2up.txt)

## From 27 triangles to 20

The starting tile comes from our previous construction in which an eight-triangle marked carrier was realized as a 53-triangle outline and reduced by two axial folds. The previous submission contains a checked three-ring witness and an UNSAT certificate for the resulting 27-iamond. Its interior edge-neighbor positions give physically observed motions for moving portions of that outline. For neighboring placements A and B, our program records the relative map A⁻¹B in the coordinate frame of tile A. We count neighbors across centers in levels zero, one, and two of the hole-free patch. There are 18 distinct directed relative maps for this parent. [Attempt 20](https://github.com/Layr-Labs/heesch/tree/9f248f48049a7fad417a3de8e10ed2dfc7cf7866/submission)

Our initial outline bank comes from convex pieces of the parent or one of its compact subshapes. A convex triangular-lattice polygon has boundary segments on three lattice axes. We intersect strips along all three axes and retain pieces whose convex hull has exactly their area. This tests convexity in the actual triangular geometry. We keep the remaining cells connected, require connected and hole-free reconstructed children, and discard duplicates up to rotations, reflections and legal translations. We then use observed neighbor motions to transport pieces across the exposed boundary. For comparison, some searches allowed a bounded additional lattice displacement. The two moves that recover the record tile both use their observed motions exactly.

First remove a convex seven-triangle corner of the 27-iamond. Two pieces of the remainder move simultaneously. A two-triangle piece follows the affine map `<0,-1,1,1,1,-2>`. A regular hexagon made of six triangles follows `<1,1,1,-1,0,1>`. Each relative placement occurs four times in the complete three-ring starting chart. The remaining twelve triangles and the two repositioned pieces form a connected, hole-free set of twenty cells. An ordinary grid symmetry aligns it with entry 131 of Kaplan’s table. The canonical shape digest is `76c26491708aea73618605b352d8d6d58681f20c2cf67bf9e574c8b7398412ff`. We consulted the published table after generating the bank. The scored outline’s shape and its Heesch number were already in the literature. This entry contributes the explicit geometric relation between the two finite tiles.

## Four full rings and 43 additional copies

The independent search received the reconstructed outline as input and returned 5, 12, 19, and 30 new tiles in the four successive rings, with 67 copies including the center. For comparison we transported the 66-copy Attempt 17 witness onto the literal cut outline using the explicit affine transformations in `research/data/recovered20.json`. Aligning the central copies shows that both arrangements have identical first three rings. Thirteen of the fresh fourth ring’s thirty copies coincide with Attempt 17. Twelve coincide with the earlier 67-copy arrangement from the original proof submission. The archive carries the independent fresh chart, the transported 66-copy chart and the center-aligned comparison counts. [Attempt 17](https://github.com/Layr-Labs/heesch/tree/4760bd56688f86c7f14a4fb2af648b908f8e4b09/submission)

For the fixed outer boundary of the new arrangement, 396 sites require coverage. We generated 1,792 legal placements that cover at least one required site. The first CP-SAT pass proved that at least 68 required sites must remain uncovered on this footprint. Its solution attained 68 but enclosed 38 additional cells, giving 106 charged defects. A pocket-aware pass kept 43 partial tiles. They cover 323 required sites, leave 73 required gaps, and enclose two additional pocket cells. The official charged count is 75, which gives score `4 + 321/396 = 4.810606060606…`. The packing search establishes feasibility for the charged value 75. A lower bound of 68 on direct gaps alone does not settle the best possible charged total between 68 and 75. The prior 66-copy Attempt 17 scored `4 + 318/367 = 4.866485…` under a different outside boundary. The current archive records the construction and its independently obtained arrangement at the lower score.

The official non-tiler proof bytes for the 20-iamond come from Rubenmarcus’s published submission `1526ffd8-bbb6-4b1b-a320-83dab048b686`, candidate commit `4dedf218ffe800865898102683553f365defb4b4`. Before attaching them we registered the reconstructed outline, regenerated the revision-two multilevel `F(S,5)` CNF, and matched its full SHA-256, variable count, and clause count. We also matched the uncompressed DRAT digest. The CNF has 1,272,828 variables and 11,038,715 clauses. The witness gives Hc and Hh at least four. The proof at depth five pins both to exactly four. ## Five reciprocal contact weights on the parent

The 27-iamond admits a short additional obstruction to tiling. For the possible edge neighbors of one chosen tile we enumerate every symmetry and integral lattice translation that covers at least one of its 15 edge-adjacent halo sites. We discard overlaps and pairs that enclose an area indivisible by 27. In a complete plane tiling any trapped component must consist entirely of other whole connected tiles, so extending such a pair is impossible. Using total enclosed area for the divisibility check gives a necessary test and leaves a superset of usable placements. The procedure retains 133 directed relative placements. All retained images are distinct and the list is closed under inversion. Enumerating every disjoint exact cover of the 15 halo sites yields 45 possible neighbor arrangements, or 41 distinct signed incidence profiles.

Five directed contacts receive integer weights, shown with their actual geometry in `research/diagrams/five-contacts.pdf`. Give the inverse relative pose the opposite weight and give every remaining directed contact zero. The five positive weights are 2, 1, 2, 2, and 1. Direct evaluation on the complete list gives +1 for 36 arrangements and +3 for nine. In any complete plane tiling, sum the charges over tiles whose chosen reference points lie within a square of side L. Every contact internal to the square cancels with its inverse. The number of contacts crossing the window is O(L), because the tiles have fixed diameter, positive area, disjoint interiors and bounded degree. The number of tiles of the tiling in the square is of order L², and each carries a positive charge. The two bounds contradict one another as L grows. This argument certifies non-tiling for the 27-iamond. Its exact depth three comes from the separate previously checked `F(S,4)` proof and witness. We do not transfer the five weights to the 20-iamond.

## Following the four-ring seams

We recorded 31 directed edge contacts using levels zero through two of the freshly obtained four-ring chart. We then generated further compact, unmarked 20-iamonds using 45 available convex pieces. After geometric and prior-bank deduplication, 921 new outlines came from one relocation and 21,456 from two, a total of 22,377. The archive provides the full outline table, the cells and affine motions for every modification, and the native outcomes at depths three, four and five. `research/replay.py --all-origins` reconstructs all 22,377 outlines from the described cuts and compares their registered cell sets. Including unsuccessful edits makes the search neighborhood identifiable.

At target depth five, 20,430 outlines failed the recursive contact screen and a further 1,915 were classified as isohedral tilers. The initial witness search found 25 complete fifth rings and timed out on seven geometries. Rerunning those seven with another seed yielded three additional fifth rings. All 32 difficult geometries, including every one with a five-ring patch, have explicit periodic plane-tiling certificates. For each, the archive records the triangular-lattice period sublattice and all tile motions in a quotient fundamental cell. An independent replay checks proper triangular-grid symmetries, the two site orientations and multiplicity one on every residue. The 28 five-ring patches pass the frozen witness verifier. Bounded search failures are search outcomes; only the explicit tiling certificates decide the 32 periodic cases. This family produced no finite five-ring witness.

## Reproduction and attribution

With the benchmark installed, run `python submission/research/replay.py --all-origins` and `python submission/research/check_charge.py` from the checkout. The first command rebuilds the cuts, replays both four-ring arrangements, computes the official defect, reconstructs every further outline, checks the fifth-ring witnesses and verifies the period certificates. The second exhausts local edge stars using the published 27-iamond cells and checks the area-versus-boundary obstruction. The scored part can also be read directly with `python -m heesch_verify submission/best.heesch`. The archived figures trace the construction, show the actual partial fifth ring and identify the five weighted contacts. No auxiliary code participates in remote scoring.

We reused the coverage pass and triangular pocket optimizer previously published in our 22-iamond study. The former comes from our fixed-patch coverage entry and the latter from our earlier triangular-grid packing entry. For the present work we generalized the output ring number of the pocket solver to five. Its optimization setup is recorded in `research/data/new-four-packing.log`. The native search uses Kaplan’s triangular grid and isohedral support. The construction, actual relative seam data, contact-weight obstruction, independent fourth ring, periodic certificates and descendant audit were obtained during this exploration.
