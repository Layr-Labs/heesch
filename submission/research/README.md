# The exact global fifth-ring fraction on the finite 20-hex

Thirty-eight outer tiles cover 233 of 237 required sites on mpjunior92’s finite 20-hex. Four sites remain uncovered and there are no extra enclosed sites. The charged score is \(4+233/237=4.983122362869198\ldots\), up from \(4+221/231=4.9567099567\ldots\) in our earlier construction. It equals the public score on this outline. The complete no-radius search establishes that this is the maximum fifth-ring fraction on this particular polyhex. The archive also exhibits the largest number of exterior copies possible at that fraction on the public 237-site fourth patch. [Published 20-hex and shape certificate](https://github.com/Layr-Labs/heesch/tree/0eeaa5f18bb1f272d8690011137616dd3e2b8816/submission) · [Our previous 20-hex](https://github.com/Layr-Labs/heesch/tree/beb3f9870c50fc879bd47b495aba9625f3fb33bb/submission)

The complete patch has one central copy and rings of 5, 12, 24 and 28 copies. Its occupied fourth footprint matches the publication by mpjunior92. The new 38-copy packing shares 30 tiles with that publication’s 37-copy packing. The missed required sites are `(-10,22)`, `(-7,19)`, `(12,-14)` and `(15,-17)`. In a fresh solver run we required charged defect at most four and maximized the number of outer placements. Exact cell disjointness, mandatory boundary contact and flood-filled pocket cuts gave an objective and bound of 38. The geometry and digests of both the inherited LRAT and its clause-index core are independently checked by the companion replay.

Starting at the identity center, we jointly enumerated complete rings one and two and blocked the entire occupied first footprint after each geometric solution. Exactly five first footprints admit a complete second ring. On those five, exactly five second footprints admit a complete third ring. Two distinct third footprints admit a complete fourth ring. Every enumeration ended `INFEASIBLE` after all witnesses had been excluded by occupied region. Candidate tiles were generated by aligning all twelve oriented copies of the shape against every site of the actual required boundary. The search has no additional radius restriction.

For a fixed third footprint we maximized the next boundary size \(B\) subject to a solid fourth-ring covering constraint, allowing holes when computing an upper bound. The exact relaxed maxima on the two branches were 266 and 255. With each individual bound in the model, we then searched jointly over the fourth and partial fifth rings for \(4B-237d\ge1\), where \(d\) is the union of missing required sites and enclosed empty sites. Both strict tests returned `INFEASIBLE`. This excludes every strict improvement over the scored 4/237 defect fraction within the benchmark’s conventions. The optimization record uses CP-SAT. The official LRAT certificate independently proves the shape cannot complete five full rings. The placement and pocket models follow our 15-hex search and the previously published solve-and-flood approach.
