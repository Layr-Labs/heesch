# Moving the tips of a periodic 19-hex

The parent in `parent.heesch` has 112 tiles across a center and five complete hole-free rings. We fitted the 19-cell outline to a deformation of the occupied patch through level two of Yukon submission `908a0696-f1fb-48e5-9bd9-5a301e65620a`. The fitting stage mapped each new lattice cell back through the deformation and rounded to the nearest hex cell. We then selected a common footprint and independently searched the affine placements of the resulting shape. The parent’s four-copy torus certificate is in `parent-period.json`.

The outline has four cells with at most two edge neighbors. We removed up to two cells from these tips and their immediate neighbors, requiring each nonempty removal to include a tip. We added up to two cells. Added pairs can touch separate parts of the outline, or the second addition can touch the first. We retained connected, hole-free shapes of 18–20 cells, and eliminated duplicates under all twelve lattice symmetries and translations. `tips.py` generates the 6,085 outlines using the challenge’s hole detector and canonicalizer.

A bounded native search produced eight explicit five-ring witnesses, and every one passed the benchmark’s geometry verifier. All eight admit four-copy periodic partitions. Three of the eight break the parent’s specific periodic placement. For example, `778a7d11c50a4092f029` moves two tips, leaving eight untouched residues and eight duplicate coverings under the old four placements. Its 108-tile witness has five complete rings. A new four-copy partition on a torus with period vectors `(76,0)` and `(32,1)` covers all 76 residues exactly once. Translating that block tiles the plane. The `FOUND` total counts emitted witnesses. The native search bypasses isohedral tilers and its negative categories do not establish the Heesch number of every other shape.

Activate the benchmark environment and run `python submission/research/tips/replay_tips.py` to check the five-ring patches, their corresponding edits in the parent frame, and the periodic certificates. `research/tips/patch5/` holds the eight witnesses. The primary scoring witness is the 10-cell polyiamond at `submission/best.heesch`.
