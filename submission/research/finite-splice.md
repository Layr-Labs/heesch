# Boundary splices from finite polyhex parents

The donor library comprises 258 Hc = 3 polyhexes and six Hc = 4 polyhexes. Five four-ring donors had already been tested in the index-lift experiments. The sixth was the 13-cell Kaplan polyhex in attempt 12. The 17-hex table and other seed tables are available from [Kaplan’s Heesch project](https://cs.uwaterloo.ca/~csk/heesch/). The local 20-hex finite examples were the Hc = 4 and Hc = 3 cases from earlier independently certified attempts. None of the donors was taken from a five-ring periodic witness. The operation changes the tile boundary geometrically and lets the resulting child choose its own placements.

## Exact join construction

The generator `splice.py` applies every axial orientation to every donor and registers a candidate join at each occupied donor cell. In the transformed coordinates, left/right fragments are separated by a function of y. The three runs used x ≤ 0, x ≤ floor(y/2), and x ≤ 2 for y = −1 or 0 (otherwise x ≤ 0). The last join takes a two-column detour around a tooth. The parser compares the complete two-column seam comprising sites satisfying −1 ≤ x − f(y) ≤ 0 in the two registered parents. A matching seam signature allows the left piece of one parent to be met to the right piece of the other. Both constituent pieces must have at least three cells, the seam must have at least two cells, and the result must be a connected, hole-free polyhex with 18 through 23 cells. Parent identity, placement orientation and the local anchor coordinates are retained for every sampled child. The free-shape symmetry key removes rotations, translations and reflections as well as changes of file order. The seed used to sample the available fragment pairs makes runs repeatable.

The initial per-cut draws contained 65,000 straight joins, 60,000 indented joins and 60,000 sloped joins. After collapsing canonical duplicates across runs, the three families contributed 65,000, 46,124 and 45,532 distinct children, respectively. The total was 156,656, including 88,263 in the 18–20-cell proof band. `pool.txt` and `pool-origins.jsonl` contain this sample. Every pair came from two distinct finite source tiles. The library and the number of pose/register choices admit substantially more fragment pairs than this sample.

## Geometry and periodic certificates

A necessary local surround screen at depth five and work budget 3,000 labeled 121,784 constructions EXCLUDED, 30,340 UNKNOWN, and 4,532 SURVIVED. We passed every UNKNOWN and SURVIVED case to the native five-ring search. We also sent all 6,742 children having at least one Hc = 4 parent directly, including any such children already excluded by the first screen. In all, 39,575 distinct candidate shapes received direct search. The strongest result per shape, across seeds when duplicated, gave 32,495 NO_DEEP_CONTACT, 6,918 ISOHEDRAL, 21 EXHAUSTED, six TIMEOUT, and 135 FOUND. Actual witnesses were checked individually. Labels such as EXHAUSTED are local search outcomes and are not benchmark UNSAT certificates.

The frozen witness verifier validated five coronas in every one of the 135 FOUND cases. Independent Hermite-normal-form exact covers produced tiling certificates for all 135. The smallest periods in the tested order used two copies for eight shapes, three for eight, four for 86, six for 20, eight for 11, ten for one, and twelve for one. The occupied cells in a certificate reduce to each residue exactly once. The period translations therefore cover the entire plane. All six TIMEOUT cases were resolved as periodic as well. These certificates appear in `five-periods.jsonl` and `timeout-periods.jsonl`, including orientation indices and an explicit basis for the period lattice.

The four-ring follow-up tested 36,644 candidates, giving 32,808 NO_DEEP_CONTACT, 3,733 ISOHEDRAL, 75 FOUND, 25 EXHAUSTED and three TIMEOUT. Of the 75 checked four-ring geometries, 71 overlapped with the five-ring witnesses. The remaining four also had exact periods, with two to four copies. All three timeouts had independently verified periods. The archive `four-periods.jsonl` records the full cover for each four-ring geometry. Thus no new finite Hc ≥ 4 witness arose from this geometric splice search.

## Inverse outline fitting

`chart.py` solves for the unmarked outline and selected translations of its neighbors simultaneously. Each possible outline cell has a Boolean variable. Once a copy chooses a translation, conjunctions of that choice with the outline variables determine which world cells the copy occupies. At most one enabled copy can claim each world cell. For every occupied inner cell, its six neighboring sites must be covered at the next stage. We perturbed the chart centers by a small affine drift and let each outer-ring copy choose among its shifted position and six neighboring translations. The central outline can differ from its donor by up to four removed cells and four additional cells. Each candidate is checked for connectedness, simple topology and exact corona geometry with the frozen verifier. This fit is restricted to a one-cell envelope of the seed, but the actual outline is an explicit solution variable. Different first-ring surroundings from the same finite four-ring witness can yield the same or different fitted shapes.

The depth-two stage tested 77 finite charts under five finite affine drifts. Each of the 385 exact models was infeasible after requiring a changed outline, fixed interior poses and independently moving second-ring poses. This result concerns just these restricted charts and the one-cell deformation envelope. The one-ring stage supplies actual verifier-approved fitted outlines, which are then separately tested by the usual depth-four and depth-five placement engines and periodicity certification.

The first-ring inverse search finished across all 77 charts and five drift choices. It yielded 12,485 raw feasible models, of which 11,748 reached independent geometry verification, before duplicate canonical outlines were removed. The resulting 990 distinct, verifier-approved children ranged from 11 to 24 cells. Each entered separate depth-four and depth-five screens. Nineteen produced verified four- and five-ring patches across the sampled search seeds. Their independent complete lattice covers had four copies (16 children), six copies (one child), or eight copies (two children). The single second-pass five-ring search timeout was among those independently solved as periodic. Thus the simultaneously fitted outlines and displaced neighbors did change the geometric possibilities, yet all 19 deep shapes in this sample tiled the plane. For reproducibility, `chart1-constructions.jsonl` stores the identities of the source witness and model phase, `chart1/` contains a verified first ring on every fitted child, and `chartfive-periods.jsonl` stores the exact torus covers. No fitted child in this set qualified as a new finite Hc ≥ 4 submission.
